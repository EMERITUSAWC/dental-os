What each figure means

  • Gross production: the fees of treatment marked done in the period.
  • Net production: gross production plus discounts and write-offs, which are taken off.
  • Collected: every payment taken in the period that was not voided, from patients and insurers, including membership fees.
  • Of production collected: treatment payments divided by net production. Above 100% means old bills were paid down.
  • No-show rate: visits marked missed divided by visits done plus visits missed. Cancelled visits are counted separately.
  • Plans accepted: of the plans presented in the period, how much of the money is now accepted. Plans still deciding count as not accepted yet, so recent periods look low.
  • Chair time booked: booked minutes divided by open minutes over working days. Public holidays are not taken off.
  • Recall not overdue: of patients on recall, the share whose check-up is not past due.
