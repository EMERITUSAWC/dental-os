The ordinary API never returns clinical records. A FHIR key does: medical alerts, treatment, prescriptions and insurance, in the international FHIR R4 format at /api/fhir/R4. Make one only for a hospital, health system or program the clinic has an agreement with, and has a legal basis to share records with. Every record it reads is listed in that patient's "who looked" log.
